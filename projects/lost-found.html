Project 04 / Campus Utility

College Lost & Found

College Lost & Found is a project concept intended to help students report and discover items lost or found around their college campus.

Project Overview

The idea is to create a centralized place where campus members can share information about lost and found belongings, making it easier to reconnect people with their items.

Project Direction

  • Lost item reporting concept
  • Found item information sharing
  • Campus-focused lost-and-found experience

Development Status

College Lost & Found is currently under development. Screenshots, confirmed features, and technology details will be added as implementation progresses.
