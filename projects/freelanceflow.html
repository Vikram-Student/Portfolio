Project 02 / Workflow Management

FreelanceFlow

FreelanceFlow is a project concept designed to organize freelance work in one place, bringing client management, tasks, and payment tracking into a structured workflow.

Project Overview

The goal is to make freelance work easier to organize and manage, from handling client details to tracking tasks and payments.

Planned Focus Areas

  • Client management
  • Task and work tracking
  • Payment tracking
  • Organized freelance workflow

Development Status

FreelanceFlow is currently under development. Features and screenshots will be added as implementation progresses.
